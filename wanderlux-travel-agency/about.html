A small agency built around clear planning

WanderLux was founded to fix the two things travellers complain about most: vague pricing and generic itineraries. We do neither.

Our story

Founded on one idea: show the price first

WanderLux Travel Agency is a newly established travel service provider offering personalised holiday planning, guided tours, and customised travel packages for domestic and international destinations. We started the agency after years of watching travellers get quotes only after several phone calls — so our Trip Cost Calculator and transparent packages exist to put a real number in front of you from the very first visit.

From there, every itinerary is shaped around how you actually want to travel: fast-paced city breaks, slow island stays, group tours, or private honeymoons.

Mission

Plan honestly, travel confidently

We help every traveller understand exactly what they're paying for, before they pay for it — and we stay reachable for the whole trip, not just the booking.

Transparent pricing

Every estimate shows its working — daily rate, accommodation and travel style — before you speak to anyone.

Personal fit

No two itineraries are identical — consultants build around your budget, pace and interests.

Reliable support

A named consultant stays on your booking from first enquiry to the day you land back home.
